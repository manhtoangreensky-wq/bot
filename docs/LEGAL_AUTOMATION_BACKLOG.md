# Legal Automation Backlog

Backlog only. Not part of current Stable Revenue Bot phase.

## Future Module Name

Legal Docs Lite

## Possible Features

- Terms of service generator
- Refund policy generator
- Service contract generator
- Installation acceptance report
- Warranty policy
- Basic legal document review
- Customer agreement templates

## Possible Providers

- OpenLaw / Lexi AI
- OpenLaws API for verified legal data
- Internal templates
- Human lawyer review later

## Safety Rules

- Legal output is informational only.
- Not a replacement for licensed attorney advice.
- Do not generate high-risk legal advice without disclaimer.
- Do not store sensitive legal documents without user consent.
- Admin approval required before selling legal automation.
