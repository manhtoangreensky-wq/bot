# Multi-Platform Strategy

## Facebook

Output should include:

- title
- longer caption than TikTok
- moderate hashtags
- soft CTA
- page/group suggestion
- affiliate disclosure

## TikTok

Output should include:

- 1-2 second hook
- short caption
- trend hashtags
- product angle
- CTA through comment or bio link
- affiliate disclosure

## YouTube Shorts

Output should include:

- keyword title
- description
- hashtags
- pinned comment
- affiliate disclosure
- long-form expansion idea

## Instagram

- visual caption
- Reels hashtags
- lifestyle angle
- product/lifestyle proof

## Threads

- short story post
- discussion hook
- traffic back to the main platform

## OnlyFans / Paid Fan Platform

- legal content only
- fictional characters or real people with explicit consent only
- no deepfake of real people
- manual review required
- age and legal compliance required before implementation

## Website / Landing

- lead collection
- portfolio
- offer page
- contact form
- payment link
