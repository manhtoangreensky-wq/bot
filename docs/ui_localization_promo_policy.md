# UI Localization and Promotion Policy

Internal product note. Do not display this document in the public bot UI.

## Language rules

- Vietnamese users receive Vietnamese UI copy.
- English users receive English UI copy.
- Chinese users receive Chinese UI copy.
- Common product terms such as AI, video, prompt, logo, watermark, PDF, TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and YouTube may remain when they are clearer than a forced translation.
- Public copy must not expose provider names, model names, HTTP details, environment variables, API keys, debug text, or internal readiness status.
- Confirmation screens describe user-visible state: processing has not started, Xu has not been charged, or an eligible refund was recorded.

## Vietnam deposit promotions

- Domestic deposit promotions apply only to eligible Vietnam PayOS payments or manual transfers to a Vietnam bank account.
- Existing approved domestic promotion codes and launch bonuses remain visible in the Vietnamese promotion table.
- Deposit promotions do not apply to Zalo, MoMo, international channels, or other channels outside Vietnam PayOS/bank transfer.
- A deposit promotion is separate from member-tier discounts, birthday/loyalty benefits, campaign benefits, monthly plans, and service combos.

## International promotion policy

- International pricing and promotion screens do not advertise Vietnam domestic deposit codes or domestic launch bonuses.
- International users may receive actual member-tier service discounts and eligible birthday, loyalty, or other non-deposit benefits.
- The public English note is:
  "Deposit promotions are available only for Vietnam PayOS/bank-transfer campaigns. International users receive member-tier discounts and eligible non-deposit benefits only."

## Member benefits

- Member tier is separate from monthly plans and service combos.
- Member copy separates service discounts, birthday/loyalty benefits, deposit promotions, and campaign promotions.
- Public member screens do not expose owner/admin-only controls or operational status.

## Approved video flow copy

The approved order remains:

1. Prompt/storyboard.
2. Aspect ratio when needed.
3. Tools/add-ons.
4. Package selection.
5. Scene-count pricing.
6. Final invoice.
7. Export confirmation.

Each public screen must clearly show the information relevant to that step. Scene-count copy includes estimated duration and price. The final invoice includes aspect ratio, selected tools, package, scene count, formula, total Xu, and a clear statement that processing and charging start only after final confirmation.

This audit changes copy and policy presentation only. It does not change payment, wallet, provider, export, multi-scene rendering, voice, music, or subtitle engines.

## Future app/web backlog

Future web/app direction: visual workflow canvas with master reference, image cards, video clip cards, scene stitching, ZIP export.

This direction is backlog context only and must not appear in the public Telegram bot UI.
