# AI Growth Coach Lite

## Purpose

Use manual data from `/publish_done` and `/performance_add` so AI can suggest what to scale, fix, pause, and remix.

## Command

```text
/growth_ai
/growth_ai days=30
/growth_ai platform=tiktok
/growth_ai campaign_id=1 goal="tăng affiliate"
```
